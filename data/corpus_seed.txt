The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.
All living organisms are composed of one or more cells.
The nucleus contains the cell's genetic material and controls cellular activities.
Mitochondria generate most of the cell's supply of adenosine triphosphate used as chemical energy.
Photosynthesis is the process by which plants convert light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose.
Fire produces heat and light through chemical reaction of combustion.
Water extinguishes fire by removing heat and oxygen from the fuel.
Gravity attracts masses toward each other creating orbital mechanics in planetary systems.
The economy functions through the exchange of goods and services using currency.
Inflation reduces the purchasing power of money over time.
Love creates strong emotional bonds between individuals in social structures.
Learning requires memory consolidation and active attention.
Reasoning connects facts through logical inference and causal deduction.
Quantum entanglement links particle states instantaneously across physical distance.
Stars fuse hydrogen into helium inside high pressure nuclear cores.
Robots process sensor input to navigate complex physical environments.
Computers execute algorithms stored in memory to manipulate digital data.
Ecosystems maintain balance through food webs and nutrient cycling.
Evolution adapts populations through natural selection over generations.
Antibiotics destroy bacteria by disrupting cell wall synthesis.
Viruses replicate by hijacking host cell molecular machinery.
DNA stores biological instructions in nucleotide sequences.
Proteins fold into three dimensional shapes to catalyze biochemical reactions.
Neuron networks transmit electrical signals across synaptic gaps.
Democracy distributes governance authority through elected citizen representatives.
Philosophy explores foundational questions of existence, knowledge, and ethics.
Art expresses human emotion and aesthetic perspective through creative media.
Music structures sound frequencies and rhythm to evoke psychological resonance.
Languages encode semantic concepts into grammatical syntax for communication.
Mathematics models quantitative relationships using formal axiom systems.
Physics investigates fundamental interactions of matter, energy, space, and time.
Chemistry studies molecular composition, reactivity, and phase transitions.
Astronomy observes celestial bodies and cosmic evolution across light years.
Geology analyzes earth crust composition, plate tectonics, and rock layers.
Climate system balances solar radiation through atmospheric and oceanic circulation.
Rain falls when water vapor condenses into heavy droplets inside cloud formations.
Sunlight provides energy that drives terrestrial weather patterns and plant growth.
